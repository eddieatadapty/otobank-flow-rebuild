Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the Meta or Google campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Bedtime, the commute and English each get their own first screens; installs from your brand search and your other ads keep today’s start.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. Today your ads sell the web subscription (card or carrier billing); these flows are for the installs that open the app, or for the same ads pointed at the App Store as a test. We found no Apple Ads running for audiobook.jp in the US and saw no Japanese search data. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
